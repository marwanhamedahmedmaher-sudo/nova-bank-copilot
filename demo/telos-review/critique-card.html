Key Result
KR 1: Handle time (FCR held)
Cut average handle time on dispute, fee and card contacts by at least 1 minute per contact, without lowering first-contact resolution.
Model: an AI copilot inside Nova Bank's desktop agent console (Egypt), laid out in three columns: case queue, the conversation with the copilot's draft, and the verified customer's records. The copilot drafts a formal-Arabic reply grounded in policy and this customer's records, cites every claim, and only suggests actions that a human must click and sometimes have approved. Flow: a Franco-Arabic chat asking when a disputed EGP 1,200 will come back. Benchmark floor: EricaAssist's roughly 1 minute saved per call.

What to Reconsider
1
Every contact starts with a wait of about 16 seconds, and the agent has to trigger it.
The customer's message is already in the case on screen 02, but nothing happens until the agent clicks "Ask copilot". Screen 03 then shows only "Reading policies and this customer's records…" while the button spins. The metrics row on 04 records 15.75s for this draft. That is a quarter of the one-minute target spent idle on every contact, before the agent reads a word. The right panel already shows the dispute stage, so an impatient agent may start typing a reply of their own and lose the draft's saving.

Mariam writes "lessa mafeesh 7aga" (still nothing). The record shows EGP 1,200 credited to her account on 2026-10-02 (T00018). The draft opens with the dispute stage and last-update date. A customer who thinks she has received nothing will ask again: "is the money mine?" now, or "why was it taken back?" later. The "For you" box lists the facts but never tells the agent the key point: what the customer believes doesn't match the record.

Suggestions Tied to the KRs
9
Start the draft when the case opens, stream the reply first, and keep Send locked until the checks pass.
Run the copilot as soon as a case is assigned, since the message is already in it, so the draft is ready or nearly ready when the agent opens CASE-101. Show "Draft ready" on the queue card. Stream the Arabic reply before the claims and gloss, keep Send disabled until the guardrails pass, and turn on prompt caching for the policy documents (the row shows "cached 0" today). Keep "Ask copilot" as a regenerate button. Nothing is sent until the agent clicks.
Handle time
How did I get this?
This answers rec 1 directly. Pre-generating moves the 15.75s off the agent's clock and into queue time. Streaming means even a draft that wasn't pre-generated is readable within a few seconds, and caching cuts processing time on every request. The agent stays in control because Send stays manual and locked until the checks pass. For calls, pair this with a short capture field or a live transcript so the draft doesn't wait for the agent to finish typing.
10
Lead with the answer, explain "provisional", and say what happens next.
Change the dispute-status reply pattern to three parts. (1) "The EGP 1,200 has been in your account since 2 October as a provisional credit." (2) One line from POL-04 §5 on what makes it permanent and what reverses it, with 5 business days' notice. (3) The next step and when she'll hear back. When the customer's words contradict the record, add a one-line heads-up to "For you", for example: "Customer believes nothing happened; credit posted 10-02." Add this case to the golden eval set so the ordering is tested, not hoped for.
