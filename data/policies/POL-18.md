# POL-18 · Credit Card Fees and Interest
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Annual fee
Classic: EGP 300. Gold: EGP 750. Platinum: EGP 1,500, waived if annual card spend is at least EGP 150,000.

## §2 Late payment fee
EGP 200 if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

## §3 Minimum payment
5% of the statement balance or EGP 100, whichever is higher.

## §4 Interest
2.5% a month on the unpaid balance. No interest is charged on purchases if the full balance is paid by the due date.

## §5 Cash advances
Cash advances cost 3% of the amount, minimum EGP 50, and interest applies from the day of withdrawal.
