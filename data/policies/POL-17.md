# POL-17 · Customer Verification and Privacy
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Verification first
Before discussing any account information, the caller must pass verification: full name, the last 4 digits of their national ID, and an OTP sent to their registered mobile number.

## §2 Account holder only
Account information is shared only with the verified account holder. Spouses, relatives and employers cannot receive it without a registered power of attorney.

## §3 Never read out
Never read out full card numbers, national ID numbers or OTPs. Refer to cards by their last 4 digits.

## §4 Business accounts
For business accounts, only authorised signatories registered on the account can receive information.

## §5 Scoped tools
Agent tools and the copilot only show data for the verified customer on the open case.
