# POL-15 · Fraud and Unauthorised Transactions
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 After reporting
The customer is not liable for unauthorised transactions made after reporting the card lost or stolen.

## §2 Before reporting
For unauthorised transactions before the report, the customer's liability is capped at EGP 1,000 if they report within 2 days of noticing. If the customer shared their PIN or acted with gross negligence, they may be fully liable. The Disputes team decides liability.

## §3 OTPs and PINs
Nova Bank never asks for an OTP or PIN. If a customer shared an OTP, the agent still opens a dispute and flags the case to the Fraud team.

## §4 Suspected fraud
On suspected fraud the agent blocks the card, opens a dispute and refers the case to the Fraud team the same day.
