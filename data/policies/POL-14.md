# POL-14 · SMB Business Account Fee Schedule
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Monthly account fee
Business Starter: EGP 150 a month. Business Growth: EGP 400 a month, waived if the previous month's average balance was at least EGP 250,000.

## §2 Domestic transfers
The first 20 domestic transfers each month are free; after that each transfer costs EGP 5.

## §3 Business debit card replacement
Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged business debit card costs EGP 200. Defective and expired cards are replaced free.

## §4 Cash deposits
Branch cash deposits are free up to EGP 200,000 a month; above that, 0.1% of the amount.

## §5 Payroll service
The payroll service costs EGP 3 per employee per payroll run.

## §6 Who can ask
Only authorised signatories registered on the business account can receive account information (POL-17 §4).
