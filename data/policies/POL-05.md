# POL-05 · Dispute Stages and Timelines
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Submitted
The dispute has been received. Nova Bank acknowledges it by SMS within 2 business days.

## §2 Under review
The Disputes team is checking the transaction and the customer's evidence. This stage takes up to 10 business days.

## §3 Provisional credit
A temporary credit has been applied while the case is open. See POL-06.

## §4 Awaiting merchant
Nova Bank has sent the claim to the merchant's bank through the card network. The merchant has up to 30 days to respond (45 days for international merchants).

## §5 Resolved in favour / Declined
The decision is final at this stage. The customer is told the outcome and the reason by SMS and in the app. A customer who disagrees can log a complaint (POL-16).

## §6 Overall target
Domestic disputes are resolved within 45 days of submission and international disputes within 90 days.

## §7 What to tell the customer
Tell the customer the current stage, the date of the last update and the expected resolution date shown on the dispute record. Do not estimate any other date.
