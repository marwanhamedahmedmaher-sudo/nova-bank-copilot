# POL-19 · Personal Loans
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Product
Unsecured personal loans from EGP 10,000 to EGP 500,000, for terms of 12 to 60 months.

## §2 Admin fee
1% of the loan amount, minimum EGP 250, deducted at disbursement.

## §3 Early settlement
Settling in the first 12 months costs 2% of the outstanding amount. After 12 months, early settlement is free.

## §4 Credit decisions
Only the Credit team makes credit decisions. Agents and the copilot cannot pre-approve a loan, estimate the chance of approval or quote a personal interest rate.

## §5 Applying
Customers apply in the app and receive the decision by SMS within 3 business days.
