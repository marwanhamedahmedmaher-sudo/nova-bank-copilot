# POL-08 · Card Replacement and Delivery
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Reasons for replacement
A card can be replaced because it is lost, stolen, damaged, defective (chip or magnetic stripe failure), expiring, or because the customer changed their name.

## §2 Automatic renewal
Expiring cards are renewed automatically and sent 30 days before the expiry date, free of charge.

## §3 Delivery times
Cairo and Giza: 3 business days. Alexandria: 4 business days. Other governorates: 5 to 7 business days. Express next-business-day delivery is available in Cairo and Giza only (fee in POL-03 §3).

## §4 Activation and PIN
The new card is activated in the app or at any Nova Bank ATM. The old PIN carries over, except for cards replaced as lost or stolen, which get a new PIN by SMS.

## §5 Tracking
Replacement status is shown in the app as Ordered, Printed, With courier, Delivered. The courier calls before delivery and asks for ID.

## §6 Defective cards
Defective cards are replaced free of charge; the agent records the reason as "defective".
