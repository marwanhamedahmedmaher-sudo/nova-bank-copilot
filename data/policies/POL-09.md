# POL-09 · Standard Overdraft Coverage (personal checking)
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Opt-in
Basic and Plus customers can opt in to overdraft coverage. Without opt-in, a transaction that would overdraw the account is declined and no fee is charged.

## §2 Overdraft fee
For opted-in customers, each transaction that overdraws the account costs EGP 75, up to a maximum of 3 fees per calendar day (EGP 225).

## §3 Small-balance grace
No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by less than EGP 50 at the end of the day.

## §4 Repayment
The overdrawn balance must be repaid within 7 days. After 7 days, card payments and withdrawals are blocked until the balance is back to zero.

## §5 Planned overdraft customers
Customers with a Planned Overdraft facility are not charged per-transaction overdraft fees; POL-10 applies instead.
