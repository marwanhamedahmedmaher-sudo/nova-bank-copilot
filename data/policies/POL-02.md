# POL-02 · Personal Account Fee Schedule (superseded)
Version 2025.1 · Effective 2025-01-01 · Status: SUPERSEDED on 2026-07-01 by POL-01

Do not use this schedule for charges on or after 2026-07-01. It is kept only to explain charges made before that date.

## §1 Monthly maintenance fee
Basic: EGP 25. Plus: EGP 50. Premium: no maintenance fee.

## §2 Maintenance fee waivers
Basic: waived if the average balance in the previous calendar month was at least EGP 10,000 or a salary transfer of at least EGP 5,000 was received. Plus: waived if the average balance was at least EGP 50,000.

## §3 Statements
Electronic statements are free. Printed statements cost EGP 15 each.

## §4 Transfers
Transfers to other Nova Bank accounts are free. Transfers to other banks cost 0.1% of the amount, minimum EGP 2 and maximum EGP 20.
