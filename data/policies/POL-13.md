# POL-13 · International Transactions and Foreign Exchange
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Foreign transaction fee
Card purchases in a foreign currency, or with merchants processed outside Egypt (including online purchases priced in EGP), carry a foreign transaction fee: 3% of the amount on debit cards and 3.5% on credit cards. The fee appears as a separate "FX fee" line.

## §2 Exchange rate
Nova Bank's exchange rate on the posting date is used.

## §3 Enabling international use
International use of a debit card must be turned on in the app (Cards > International use), within the international spending limits shown there.

## §4 International ATMs
International ATM withdrawals follow POL-03 §4.
