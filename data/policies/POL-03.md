# POL-03 · Card Fee Schedule
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Card issuance
The first debit card on a personal or business account is free.

## §2 Card replacement fee
Expired or defective cards are replaced free of charge. Cards that are lost, stolen or damaged by the customer cost EGP 150 to replace on Basic and Plus. On Premium, the first replacement in any 12-month period is free and later replacements cost EGP 150. Business debit card replacement costs EGP 200 (see POL-14).

## §3 Express delivery
Express delivery (next business day) is available in Cairo and Giza only and costs EGP 100, on top of any replacement fee.

## §4 ATM withdrawals
Withdrawals at Nova Bank ATMs are free. Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in Egypt cost EGP 5 each on Basic and Plus; Premium customers get 5 free withdrawals per month at other banks' ATMs, then EGP 5 each. International ATM withdrawals cost 3% of the amount, minimum EGP 50.

## §5 PIN reissue
Reissuing a card PIN by SMS costs EGP 20.
