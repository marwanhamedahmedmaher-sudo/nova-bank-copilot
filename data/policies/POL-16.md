# POL-16 · Complaints Handling and Escalation
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Logging
Any expression of dissatisfaction is logged as a complaint with a reference (C-xxxx).

## §2 Timelines
Complaints are acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within 15 business days (Nova Bank standard).

## §3 Disagreeing with a dispute decision
If a customer disagrees with a dispute decision, the agent logs a complaint and the Disputes team reviews the case again. The second review is the bank's final decision.

## §4 External escalation
A customer who is not satisfied with the bank's final response can escalate to the Central Bank of Egypt's consumer protection channel.

## §5 What agents must do
Agents must offer the complaint route when a customer is unhappy with a decision, and must never promise a different outcome.
