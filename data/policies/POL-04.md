# POL-04 · Card Transaction Dispute Process
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 What can be disputed
The cardholder can dispute a posted transaction they do not recognise, did not authorise, were charged twice for, or paid for without receiving the goods or service. Pending transactions cannot be disputed until they post (see POL-12).

## §2 Time limit
A dispute must be opened within 60 days of the transaction's posting date. After 60 days the agent logs a complaint instead (POL-16).

## §3 How a dispute is opened
An agent opens the dispute in the disputes system after the customer passes verification (POL-17). The customer receives a dispute reference (D-xxxx) by SMS.

## §4 Card block
For transactions the customer did not authorise, the card must be blocked and replaced (POL-07).

## §5 Who decides
Only the Disputes team decides a dispute. Outcomes are: resolved in the customer's favour (the amount is refunded and any provisional credit becomes permanent) or declined (any provisional credit is reversed with 5 business days' notice).

## §6 No promises
No agent, chatbot or copilot may approve a dispute, promise an outcome, or give a refund date other than the expected resolution date on the dispute record (POL-05).
