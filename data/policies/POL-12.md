# POL-12 · Pending Transactions and Holds
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 What pending means
A pending transaction has been authorised by the merchant but not yet settled. It reduces the available balance but is not final.

## §2 How long it stays pending
Most pending transactions post within 3 business days. Pre-authorisations by hotels and fuel stations can stay pending for up to 7 days, and car rentals for up to 30 days.

## §3 Different final amount
If the final amount differs from the authorised amount, the hold is released and the final amount posts. The customer may briefly see both the hold and the final charge.

## §4 Automatic release
Holds the merchant does not claim are released automatically after 7 days (30 days for car rentals).

## §5 Disputes
A pending transaction cannot be disputed until it posts (POL-04 §1).
