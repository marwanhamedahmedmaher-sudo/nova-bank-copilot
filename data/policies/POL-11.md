# POL-11 · Fee Reversals and Goodwill Waivers
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Bank errors
A fee charged because of a bank or system error is always reversed in full. The agent submits a fee reversal request with the reason "bank error".

## §2 Goodwill waivers
Each customer can receive one goodwill waiver per 12 months, up to EGP 250, approved by a Team Leader. Waivers above EGP 250 need Operations Manager approval.

## §3 Who can apply a waiver
The copilot can suggest a waiver request. It cannot apply one. Agents cannot apply a waiver without the approval in §2.

## §4 Eligible fees
Overdraft fees, monthly maintenance fees and card replacement fees are eligible for goodwill waivers. Foreign exchange fees and interest are not.

## §5 How it appears
An approved reversal appears as "Fee reversal" within 3 business days of approval.
