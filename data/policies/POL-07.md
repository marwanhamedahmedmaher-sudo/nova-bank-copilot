# POL-07 · Lost or Stolen Card
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Block immediately
The customer can freeze the card in the app (Cards > Freeze), or the agent blocks it during the call. Blocking is free and takes effect immediately.

## §2 Liability after reporting
The customer is not liable for unauthorised transactions made after the card was reported lost or stolen. For transactions before the report, see POL-15.

## §3 No unblocking
A card blocked as lost or stolen cannot be unblocked. A replacement card with a new card number is issued.

## §4 Fees and delivery
The replacement fee follows POL-03 §2 and delivery times follow POL-08.

## §5 Recurring payments
The customer must update any subscriptions or recurring payments that used the old card number.
