# POL-06 · Provisional Credit
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Unrecognised or unauthorised transactions
For disputed transactions up to EGP 5,000, a provisional credit for the disputed amount is applied within 10 business days of submission while the case is open.

## §2 Larger amounts
Disputes above EGP 5,000 do not receive provisional credit. The amount is returned only if the Disputes team resolves the case in the customer's favour.

## §3 Duplicate charges
For duplicate charges, provisional credit is applied within 5 business days of submission.

## §4 Reversal
If the dispute is declined, the provisional credit is reversed. The customer is told by SMS at least 5 business days before the reversal.

## §5 How it appears
Provisional credit appears in the transaction list as "Provisional credit D-xxxx".
