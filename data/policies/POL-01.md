# POL-01 · Personal Account Fee Schedule
Version 2026.2 · Effective 2026-07-01 · Status: current (supersedes POL-02)

## §1 Monthly maintenance fee
The monthly maintenance fee is charged on the 1st of each month for the previous month, at the rate in effect on the charge date. Basic: EGP 35. Plus: EGP 60. Premium: no maintenance fee.

## §2 Maintenance fee waivers
Basic: the fee is waived if the average balance in the previous calendar month was at least EGP 10,000, or if a salary transfer of at least EGP 5,000 was received in that month. Plus: the fee is waived if the average balance in the previous calendar month was at least EGP 50,000. Waivers are applied automatically; no request is needed.

## §3 Statements
Electronic statements are free. Printed statements cost EGP 25 each.

## §4 Transfers
Transfers to other Nova Bank accounts are free. Transfers to other banks through the Nova app cost 0.1% of the amount, minimum EGP 2 and maximum EGP 20.

## §5 Early account closure
Closing an account within 6 months of opening costs EGP 100.

## §6 Fee changes
Nova Bank notifies customers of fee changes at least 30 days before they take effect, by SMS and in-app message. The changes in this version (maintenance fees and printed statements) were notified on 2026-06-01 and took effect on 2026-07-01. Charges before 2026-07-01 follow POL-02.
