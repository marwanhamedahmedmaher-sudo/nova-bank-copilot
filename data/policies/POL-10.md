# POL-10 · Planned Overdraft Facility
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Status: current

## §1 Eligibility and approval
A Planned Overdraft is a credit facility for Plus and Premium customers with at least 12 months' tenure and a salary transfer. The limit is up to 2 times the monthly salary, maximum EGP 100,000. Only the Credit team approves a facility or its limit. Agents and the copilot cannot approve it or tell a customer they qualify.

## §2 Interest
Interest is 24% a year on the amount used, calculated daily and charged monthly.

## §3 No per-transaction fees
Planned Overdraft customers are not charged the per-transaction fee in POL-09 §2 while they stay within their limit.

## §4 Going over the limit
For any amount above the planned limit, the fees in POL-09 §2 apply.
